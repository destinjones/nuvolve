Founding shops: ten companies, free for a year

Nuvolve is new, and it should be shaped by people who run routes, not by a spec. We're looking for ten pest control companies to use it for real, free for a year, in exchange for honest feedback.

What you get

  • Nuvolve free for 12 months, every feature, every route
  • Your data moved in for you, from GorillaDesk, FieldRoutes, PestPac or a spreadsheet
  • A direct line to the founder
  • A real say in what gets built next

What we ask

  • Run your real routes on Nuvolve for at least 90 days
  • A 20-minute call every two weeks for the first three months
  • Tell us what breaks, especially on rain days, callbacks and renewals
  • If it works for you, a short testimonial we can share (only if you're happy to, and only the words you approve)

In writing, before anything starts

The founding shop agreement answers the questions you should ask before switching software: your free year starts when your first route runs, not when you sign up. After it, you pay $39 per route a month, locked through month 36, and only if you choose to continue: no card is taken during the free year. You can leave at any time, including in month four, with no fee and all your data. Nothing about your company is quoted publicly without your written OK on the exact words.

What happens after you apply

  1. You get an email to confirm your address. Click the link so we know it was you.
  2. We read your application ourselves and reply within two business days.
  3. A 15-minute call to see whether Nuvolve fits how you work. If it doesn't, we'll say so.
  4. You read the agreement. If you're in, we email you the founding shop agreement with your details. No card, nothing to pay.
  5. We move your data in with you and you run a few routes side by side with your old system. Your free year starts the day your first real route runs.

If the founding spots are full, you're first in line for the next batch, and we'll tell you roughly when that is.

Apply, or join the waitlist

Accounts open in small batches so every new company gets real help. If the founding spots are full, you'll be first in line when the next batch opens.

By giving a phone number, you agree that Nuvolve may call or text you at that number about your request and the founding shops program, including with automated texts. Consent isn't required to buy anything. Message and data rates may apply; message frequency varies. Reply STOP to stop texts, HELP for help. See the Privacy Policy.
