Founding shops: ten companies, free for a year

Nuvolve is new, and it should be shaped by people who run routes, not by a spec. We're looking for ten pest control companies to use it for real, free for a year, in exchange for honest feedback.

What you get

  • Nuvolve free for 12 months, every feature, every route
  • Your data moved in for you, from GorillaDesk, FieldRoutes, PestPac or a spreadsheet
  • A direct line to the founder
  • A real say in what gets built next

What we ask

  • Run your real routes on Nuvolve for at least 90 days
  • A 20-minute call every two weeks for the first three months
  • Tell us what breaks, especially on rain days, callbacks and renewals
  • If it works for you, a short testimonial we can share (only if you're happy to)

Apply, or join the waitlist

Accounts open in small batches so every new company gets real help. If the founding spots are full, you'll be first in line when the next batch opens.
