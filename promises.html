What Nuvolve won't do

In this industry, "do you resell my leads?" is a real question. Here are the answers, in writing.

We won't sell or share your leads

Leads that come in through your booking form, your phone line, your door-to-door reps or your website are yours. We don't sell them, share them with other pest companies, or send them to a lead marketplace.

We won't market to your customers

Your customers hear from you, never from us. We don't email them, advertise to them, or use their information for anything but running your account.

We won't take a cut of your payments

Your customers pay into your own Stripe account. Stripe charges its normal processing fee; Nuvolve adds nothing on top.

We won't run ads in your app or portal

No ad network, no "sponsored" products in your technicians' app, no offers shown on your customers' pay page.

We won't hold your data hostage

Download everything any time, free, in spreadsheets any other system can import. No exit fee, no "request an export" form. How leaving works.

We won't lock you into a contract

Month to month. Cancel online in a few clicks, without calling anyone.

We won't train AI on your data

Your data and your customers' messages aren't used to train AI models, by us or the AI provider we use.

We won't sell your business data

Not your customer list, not your prices, not your route density, not to competitors, buyers, brokers or data companies. If you're selling your company, you decide what to share.

These promises are written into section 13 of the Terms of Service and the Data Processing Addendum, which are drafts awaiting a lawyer's review.
