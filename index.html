Photos & signatures

Proof of service on every stop

The tech takes before and after photos and any problem they find, and the customer signs on the phone. Each one is stamped with the time on the phone and where the phone was, and lands on the service report, the customer's pay page and the commercial audit binder.

  • Rules per service or for commercial accounts: how many photos, a before shot, a signature or a reason no one could sign

  • Works with no signal: photos and the signature wait on the phone and send themselves
Routes & dispatch

The fastest order, kept to the promised window

One click orders a tech's day by drive time and keeps every promised time window. Dispatch shows every truck on a live map with its next stop and arrival time, and a sick day reshuffles stops to techs who hold the right license.

Payroll & QuickBooks

Pay the techs right, keep the books clean

Hourly, production, salary and commission with the time clock and overtime, pay runs you approve, and invoices, payments and payroll posted to QuickBooks.

Sales

From the knock to a signed contract

A pipeline board for leads and quotes, door-to-door reps on a map with territories, contracts signed at the door with the 3-day right to cancel, and commissions that follow.

Callbacks

Know which tech's stops keep needing a trip back

A callback is booked against the service it follows up on, free inside that service's re-treat window. You see the callback rate by tech, service and month, repeat addresses and what free re-treats cost, and production pay skips a callback within 14 days.

Collections

Declined autopay gets chased for you

A declined card is retried on a schedule, the customer gets a link to put in a new card at each step, and the office is told when it's past due. Cards are refreshed by the card network's account updater, and expiring cards get a notice before they fail.

Texting compliance

TCPA and 10DLC, handled

Consent is recorded per channel with where it came from, STOP, START and HELP work in English and Spanish, texts wait out quiet hours in the customer's time zone, and each branch shows its A2P 10DLC brand and campaign status.

Pesticide records

Every application, ready for the state

EPA registration numbers, lot and expiration, the mix and a dilution calculator on the ticket, tank mixes, weather and the applicator. Restricted-use checks run before a stop can close, and records export in the Texas, Florida and California formats.

The demo

The real app, answering from a recording

What you're clicking

This is Nuvolve's own app, page for page. Nuvolve normally runs on a server with a database. For this site, a seeded demo company was run on the real server and every answer it gave was saved. A small helper in your browser plays those answers back, so every page, filter and customer opens without a server.

What it can't do

Adding customers, scheduling and completing stops and taking payments really work, but they're saved in your browser only, and Reset demo starts over. Settings, documents and outside services like Stripe don't save. The demo's calendar stays on the day it was recorded, so today's routes always line up. To run it for real, the app starts with one command on any server with Node.js.
