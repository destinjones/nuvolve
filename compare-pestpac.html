Nuvolve vs PestPac

PestPac by WorkWave has served pest control companies for a long time and is built for scale, especially commercial accounts. Nuvolve is built for the owner-operator who wants everything without the enterprise sale.

Pick PestPac if

  • You run a large commercial book with heavy IPM, audit and multi-unit work
  • You want smart traps, vehicle GPS tracking and general-ledger accounting from one vendor
  • You have the staff to configure a modular system and want a large, established vendor

Pick Nuvolve if

  • You want to start this week, not after a sales process and an implementation project
  • You want a published price, $39 per route a month, with nothing sold as an extra module
  • You want commercial sites, stations and an inspection binder alongside a residential route, in one simple app

Where PestPac is stronger today

  • Depth for large commercial operations, including smart trap hardware, auditor reports and general-ledger accounting.
  • Scale and history. A large vendor with many years of customers. Nuvolve is new.
  • Integrations and services like marketing and direct mail from the same company.

Switching from PestPac

Nuvolve's importer reads PestPac exports (location and bill-to codes, "Last, First" names, directions, balances), catches duplicates, and lets you undo the import in one click. Photos, attachments and signed documents stay behind, because PestPac's exports don't carry them; save the ones you need first.
