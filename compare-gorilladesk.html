Nuvolve vs GorillaDesk

GorillaDesk is a well-liked, simple tool for small pest shops, and like Nuvolve it publishes its prices. The difference is mostly in what's included and how far each one goes as you grow.

Pick GorillaDesk if

  • You run one or two routes, mostly residential, and want the simplest possible setup
  • You want an established product with a long list of reviews and a native mobile app
  • You don't need termite contracts, commercial sites, payroll or door-to-door tools

Pick Nuvolve if

  • You want one price per route with no plan tiers to outgrow
  • You do termite work (warranties, renewals, bait stations, WDI reports) or commercial accounts with many sites
  • You want payroll, commissions and QuickBooks in the same place as your routes
  • You might add a branch or buy another company's book later

Where GorillaDesk is stronger today

  • Track record. Years in the market and many reviews from small operators. Nuvolve is new.
  • Simplicity. If you'll never need termite, commercial or payroll features, a smaller tool can be easier to learn.
  • App store presence. Nuvolve's tech app is a home-screen web app, not a store download yet.

Switching from GorillaDesk

Export your customers and service history from GorillaDesk and drop the files into Nuvolve's importer. It recognizes GorillaDesk's columns (account numbers, location notes, billing addresses), matches reports that name customers instead of IDs, flags duplicates, and can be undone in one click.
