Data Processing Addendum

This addendum is part of the Terms of Service. It covers the personal information a pest control company ("Customer") puts into Nuvolve about its own customers, staff and contacts, which Nuvolve CRM ("Nuvolve") processes on the Customer's behalf.

1. Roles

For Customer Personal Data, the Customer is the controller (in California terms, the "business") and Nuvolve is the processor (the "service provider"). Nuvolve processes Customer Personal Data only to provide, secure, support and improve the service, and only on the Customer's documented instructions. The Terms, this addendum and the Customer's use and settings of the service are those instructions.

2. What is processed

PeopleThe Customer's own customers and their contacts (homeowners, tenants, property managers, site contacts), the Customer's staff and applicators, and leads.
Kinds of dataNames, service and billing addresses, phone numbers, emails, service history and notes, photos, signatures, invoices, payment records (card brand and last four only), messages, technician location while the app is open, and license records for staff.
Access notesNotes a technician needs to do the work, such as gate codes, lockbox codes, pet warnings and where to park. These are expected in Nuvolve and are protected like all other Customer Personal Data.
Sensitive dataNuvolve is not designed for sensitive categories: health or medical information (including a resident's allergies or chemical sensitivities beyond what the technician needs to know), Social Security and other government ID numbers, bank account and full card numbers, and passwords to anything other than Nuvolve. The Customer agrees not to put them in free-text fields, notes or photos. A short safety note such as "resident asks for low-odor products" is service information, not health data.
How longFor the length of the subscription, then until the Customer asks for deletion (see section 8).

3. Nuvolve's commitments

  • No selling or sharing. Nuvolve won't sell Customer Personal Data, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for any purpose outside the business relationship, including to train AI models.
  • No combining. Each Customer's data is kept in its own database and isn't combined with another Customer's data or with data Nuvolve collects elsewhere, except as the law allows a service provider to.
  • Confidentiality. Anyone at Nuvolve with access to Customer Personal Data is bound to keep it confidential, and only gets access when they need it to run or support the service.
  • Security. Nuvolve keeps the measures described on the security page in place, or ones at least as protective.
  • Compliance. Nuvolve will tell the Customer if it believes an instruction breaks the law, or if it can no longer meet its obligations under applicable privacy law.

4. Subprocessors

The Customer authorizes Nuvolve to use the subprocessors on the subprocessors page. Nuvolve has a written agreement with each that protects the data at least as well as this addendum. Nuvolve will give at least 30 days' notice (by email to the account owner and on that page) before adding or replacing a subprocessor. If the Customer objects on reasonable data-protection grounds and we can't resolve it, the Customer may cancel and get a refund of any prepaid fees for the time after the change. Nuvolve remains responsible for its subprocessors.

5. Helping with requests

If a person asks Nuvolve to see, correct or delete their information, Nuvolve will pass the request to the Customer, not answer it directly, unless the law requires otherwise. Nuvolve will help the Customer answer requests, mainly through the tools already in the app (editing records, full export, deleting customers).

6. Security incidents

If Nuvolve confirms unauthorized access to Customer Personal Data, it will notify the Customer without undue delay and within 72 hours of confirming it, say what is known and what is being done, and keep the Customer updated. Nuvolve will help the Customer meet any duty to notify regulators or the people affected. Nuvolve follows a written incident response plan, and after an incident it writes a summary of what happened, the cause and what changed, and shares it with affected Customers.

7. Audits

On request, once a year, Nuvolve will answer a reasonable written security questionnaire and provide its latest security review. Nuvolve keeps standard answers ready so this is quick; it doesn't yet hold a SOC 2 report or an outside penetration test, and will say so plainly in its answers. Where the law requires more, the parties will agree on a reasonable audit at the Customer's cost.

8. Deletion and return

The Customer can download all its data at any time (see Your data). When the Customer asks for deletion after canceling, Nuvolve deletes the Customer's database and its backups, including the off-site copy, age out within 30 days.

9. Location

Customer Personal Data is stored in the United States. Some subprocessors may process it elsewhere as listed on the subprocessors page.

10. Order of precedence

If this addendum and the Terms disagree about personal data, this addendum wins.

To request a countersigned copy of this addendum, email support@nuvolvecrm.com.
