What we believe

  • The owner who still runs a route deserves the same tools as a 20-truck company, at a price that makes sense for one truck.
  • Prices belong on the website, not behind a demo call.
  • Your data is yours. Export it any time, free, even after you leave. What we won't ever do.
  • Software should be honest about what it can't do yet. That's why there's a public roadmap and a changelog.

"What if you get hit by a bus?"

A fair question for any small software company, and one we'd rather answer before you ask.

  • You can always leave with everything. The owner can download every customer, visit, invoice and photo at any time, in spreadsheets any other system can import.
  • Nuvolve can run without us. It's a single Node.js app with SQLite databases. It can be installed on a server you control with one command, so your company can keep running it even if Nuvolve the company doesn't.
  • Backups are off-site and checked daily, as described on the security page.

The full continuity plan (source code escrow and how a hand-over would work) is being finalized and will be linked here.
