How it's worked out

  • Nuvolve: routes × $39.
  • Software: what you pay now minus what Nuvolve would cost. If you use several tools (scheduling, texting, e-signature, payroll), add them all up.
  • Office time: hours a week × the share saved × 4.33 weeks × the hourly cost.
  • Recurring revenue kept: monthly cancels × the share kept × a customer's monthly value. That's one month of revenue from customers kept that month; it keeps adding up each month they stay.

The starting numbers are examples, not results from real Nuvolve customers. We don't have published customer results yet, so we won't put a number in your head that we can't back up. Change every box to match your company.
