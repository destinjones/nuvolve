Service Level Agreement

What Nuvolve commits to on uptime and support, and what you get if we miss. This is part of the Terms of Service for every paying account.

1. Uptime: 99.5% each month

The Nuvolve app (the office app, the technician app and the customer pay page) will be available at least 99.5% of each calendar month. That allows about 3 hours and 40 minutes of downtime in a 30-day month.

  • Downtime means the app can't be logged into or can't load the schedule, a route or an invoice, for everyone in your company, as measured by our outside monitor, which checks every minute.
  • Not counted: planned maintenance announced at least 48 hours ahead on the status page and done between 10pm and 5am Central (no more than 4 hours a month); outages of outside services we don't run (phone carriers, Stripe, Google, Intuit) where Nuvolve itself is up; problems with your internet or devices; and features in beta.
  • The technician app keeps working offline. A tech who has loaded the day's route can keep completing stops without signal, and they sync when the connection comes back.

4. Your data if things go wrong

  • Backups: every night, with a copy stored off-site, checked automatically every day. Restoring from the latest backup loses at most one day of changes.
  • Recovery target: after a full server loss, we aim to have the service back within 8 hours, from the latest off-site backup.
  • Export: the owner can download everything at any time, including during a paused account.

5. Changes

We won't lower these commitments for an account during a month it has already paid for.
