Texas pest control use records: what to keep and for how long

By Destin Jones, founder of Nuvolve, who builds it for owners who still run a route · Published November 1, 2026

In Texas, structural pest control use records are governed by Texas Administrative Code, Title 4, §7.144, enforced by the Texas Department of Agriculture's Structural Pest Control Service. Here's what it requires, in plain words.

The short version: keep an accurate record of every pesticide and pest control device use for two years, at your business location, and hand it over when TDA asks, whether they ask in writing or out loud.

Who is responsible

The responsible certified applicator (or certified noncommercial applicator) must make sure the records are correct and kept. The records stay at the business location, or at the employer's premises for a noncommercial applicator.

What every record must include

The rule says records must include at least:

  1. The customer's billing name and address (or the employer, for a noncommercial applicator)
  2. The service address where the pesticide or device was used
  3. The names of the pesticides and their EPA registration numbers, or the devices used
  4. The total amount of each ready-to-use pesticide applied
  5. The devices used and how many of each
  6. For products mixed with water or another material: the mixing rate and total amount applied, or the percentage of active ingredient and total amount applied
  7. The target pest or purpose
  8. The date of use
  9. The name and license number of each person who applied, supervised or was being trained, and the business's TPCL number

Extra items for termite work

  • The area treated per site, in the right unit (for example square feet), except for bait applications
  • For a physical barrier: its size in square or linear feet, plus a diagram of the installation
  • For commercial pre-construction treatments (other than baits, wood-applied products or physical barriers): the number of application tanks used, each tank's capacity in gallons, and the start and stop time of the treatment

How Nuvolve keeps these for you
