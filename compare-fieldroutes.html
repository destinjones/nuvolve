Nuvolve vs FieldRoutes

FieldRoutes is one of the most capable platforms in pest control, and for some companies it's the right answer. Here's how to tell whether you're one of them.

Pick FieldRoutes if

  • You run a large door-to-door sales program and want its sales tooling
  • You have many branches and want an enterprise vendor with a big support team
  • You want to be inside the ServiceTitan family of products
  • A quote-based price and a sales process are fine for you

Pick Nuvolve if

  • You still run a route yourself and want software you can set up in an evening
  • You want to see the price before you talk to anyone: $39 per route a month
  • You want to try it free for 14 days without a card or a demo call
  • You want month to month, every feature included, and a free full export any time

Where FieldRoutes is stronger today

  • Scale and track record. Thousands of companies, years of edge cases, and a large support and onboarding team. Nuvolve is new.
  • Ecosystem. Being part of ServiceTitan brings connected products and more integrations than Nuvolve has today.
  • App store presence. Nuvolve's technician app is a web app you add to the home screen, not a store download yet.

Switching from FieldRoutes

Export your customer, subscription and appointment reports from FieldRoutes, and Nuvolve's importer recognizes FieldRoutes' column names, catches duplicates, and lets you undo the whole import with one click. We'll help you do it; book a 15-minute call and bring your questions.
