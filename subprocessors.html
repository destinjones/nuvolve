Subprocessors

Every outside service that may handle data to run Nuvolve, what it gets, and why. We give at least 30 days' notice here and by email to account owners before adding or replacing one, as the Data Processing Addendum says.

Connections a company turns on itself

These only receive data when a company connects its own account, and they act under the company's own agreement with them, not as Nuvolve's subprocessors.

  • Stripe (the company's own account): customer payments, saved cards and autopay
  • QuickBooks Online (Intuit): invoices, payments and payroll entries the company chooses to sync
  • Google Business Profile: reading and replying to the company's reviews
  • Zapier and webhooks: whatever events the company sends there

Lookups that carry no personal data

  • Have I Been Pwned: checks whether a new password has appeared in a known breach. Only the first five characters of the password's hash are sent, never the password or the email.
  • EPA pesticide product label lookup, and Esri map tiles: public data, nothing about your customers is sent.

Want an email when this list changes? Write to support@nuvolvecrm.com. Account owners are told automatically.
