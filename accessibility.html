Accessibility

Nuvolve should work for everyone who runs a pest control company, works a route, or pays a bill on a phone, including people who use a screen reader, keyboard, magnification or voice control. Here is where we are today, plainly, including what isn't there yet.

Our goal

We're working toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA, for the office app, the technician app, the customer pay page and portal, the booking form, and this website. We haven't had an outside audit yet, so we don't claim to meet it.

What's in place

  • Pages are built from real headings, lists, tables, labels and buttons, so screen readers can find their way around.
  • Forms have visible labels, and errors are written out in words, not only shown in color.
  • Dialogs can be closed with the Escape key, and the search box opens with the / key. We haven't yet tested every screen with a keyboard alone.
  • The technician app, the customer pay page and the booking form are built for phones first and follow the phone's text size.
  • The customer portal, booking form and technician app work in English and Spanish.

What we know isn't accessible yet

  • Maps. The dispatch map, route map and door-to-door map aren't usable with a screen reader. The technician's route and the dispatch board also list the stops in order; the door-to-door map has no list version yet.
  • Drawn signatures. The signature pad needs a finger, mouse or stylus. A customer who can't sign that way can ask the company to record that they agreed another way.
  • Charts and graphs (including termite graphs) don't yet have full text descriptions.
  • PDFs made from documents may not be fully tagged for screen readers.
  • Some colors in the soft grey theme may not meet contrast guidelines; the bright white theme has stronger contrast.

VPAT and accessibility reports

We don't have a VPAT (Accessibility Conformance Report) yet. If your organization needs one, for example a city, school district, REIT or property manager, email us and we'll tell you honestly where we are and when one will be ready. We won't send a report we haven't tested against.

Tell us what's in the way

If something in Nuvolve is hard or impossible for you to use, email support@nuvolvecrm.com, and tell us the page and what happened. We'll reply within five business days, and help you get it done another way in the meantime.
