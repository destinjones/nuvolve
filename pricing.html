Questions

Do I pay for office staff?

No. You pay for people who run routes. Office reps, managers and sales reps who don't run a route are free, and so are as many logins as you need for them.

Is there a contract?

No. You pay month to month and can cancel any time from Settings, under Payments & plan.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Your account pauses until you subscribe. Nothing is deleted, and the owner can still download everything from the account.

Can I leave with my data?

Yes, always, for free. See Your data for what's in the download and how leaving works.
