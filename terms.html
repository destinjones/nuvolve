Terms of Service

The agreement between Nuvolve CRM ("Nuvolve", "we") and the pest control company that signs up ("you"). Written in plain English on purpose. Where a word could mean two things, the plainer meaning wins.

1. What you're signing up for

Nuvolve is software for running a pest control company: scheduling, routes, the technician phone app, billing, customer messages and the rest of what's listed on the pricing page. You get access to it for as long as your subscription is active. You don't buy the software, and we don't buy your business data.

The person who signs up must be able to bind the company, and must be at least 18. You're responsible for everyone you give a login to, and for keeping logins private.

United States only. Nuvolve is offered to businesses based in the United States, for work done in the United States. The texting, records and tax features are built for US rules.

Your licenses

You must hold, and keep current, every license, certification and registration your work needs: your company's pest control business license, your applicators' and technicians' licenses, any category endorsements, and any registrations for the states and cities you work in. Nuvolve isn't a pest control company, doesn't hold a pest control license, and doesn't supervise your applicators. Nuvolve can remind you when a license or training is about to expire, but keeping them current is your job (see section 7).

2. Price, billing and automatic renewal

Your subscription renews automatically every month until you cancel. Each month we charge the card on file $39 for each route you run that month (at least one), plus any sales tax we're required to collect. You can cancel at any time in Settings, under Payments & plan, online, in a few clicks, without calling anyone.

  • Free trial. The trial lasts 14 days and needs no card. When it ends, nothing is charged unless you add a card and choose to continue.
  • What a route is. A route is a person on your team who runs a day of stops in a billing month, as defined on the pricing page. Office staff, managers and sales reps are not routes.
  • Price changes. We'll email the account owner at least 30 days before a price change takes effect. If you don't want the new price, cancel before it starts.
  • Taxes. Prices don't include sales tax or similar taxes. Some states tax software subscriptions and some don't, and we're still working out which states we must collect in. Where we're required to collect tax from you, it's added to your bill as a separate line. If we start collecting in your state, we'll tell you at least 30 days before the first bill that includes it.
  • Failed payments. If a charge fails, our payment processor emails you and retries. If it still can't be collected, the account is paused until it's paid. A paused account can still download all its data.

3. Canceling

  • Cancel any time in Settings, under Payments & plan. It takes effect at the end of the month you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.
  • We don't give partial refunds for the rest of a month you've already started, except where the law requires it or the SLA gives a credit.
  • After canceling, the owner can still log in and download everything, free. To have your company's data deleted, the owner emails us and we delete it; backups, including the off-site copy, age out within 30 days after that. See Your data.

4. Your data

Your business data, including your customers' names, addresses, service history and payments, is yours. You give us permission to store and process it only to run Nuvolve for you, keep it safe, back it up and support you. We don't sell it, rent it, or use it to market to your customers. The Data Processing Addendum is part of these terms and says this in the formal way: you are the controller of your customers' personal information, and we are your processor.

You're responsible for having the right to put your customers' information into Nuvolve and to contact them, including getting any consent the law requires before texting or calling them.

5. Payments to you

Money your customers pay goes through your own Stripe account, not ours. Stripe's terms govern those payments. Nuvolve doesn't hold your money and takes no cut of it. Card numbers are entered on Stripe's pages and never reach Nuvolve.

6. AI features (the receptionist, Ask Nuvolve, suggestions)

Some features use AI to draft replies, answer customers' texts, suggest bookings or answer questions about your numbers. These features are AI-assisted, and a person on your team can always check, change or undo what they do.

  • AI can be wrong. It can misunderstand a customer, suggest the wrong time, or describe a price incorrectly. You decide which AI features are on, what they're allowed to do, and what prices and services they may offer.
  • A price the AI quotes isn't binding on you unless you've set it as a price it may quote. Bookings it makes appear in your schedule and inbox, where your team can confirm or change them.
  • You're responsible for the messages sent from your account, including ones an AI feature drafted or sent on your behalf under the settings you chose.
  • We don't use your data or your customers' messages to train AI models, and we choose AI providers who agree not to either. See Subprocessors.
  • The receptionist says it's automated. California and other states require telling people when they're talking to a bot. The AI receptionist's first text in every conversation says it's an automated assistant, and it answers truthfully if asked. You agree not to set it up, or instruct it, to say or suggest it's a person.
  • Call recording. Nuvolve doesn't record phone calls. If you record calls on your own phones or phone system, you're responsible for giving notice and getting consent wherever the law requires it, including states that need every person on the call to agree. Nuvolve can play a recording notice before a call rings through to you; turning that on is your choice.
  • Texting consent. The booking form and the customer portal ask your customers for consent to texts and keep a record of what they agreed to. Those records help you, but you remain responsible for having consent before you text or call someone (see section 4).

7. Reminders and renewals are best-effort

Nuvolve sends reminders: appointment texts, payment reminders, license and training expiry warnings, and termite warranty and contract renewal notices. We work hard to make them reliable, and the app shows what was sent and what wasn't. But texts and emails can be delayed, filtered or lost by carriers and inboxes, and a customer's contact details can be wrong.

Reminders help you keep track. They don't replace it. You remain responsible for renewing termite warranties and contracts on time, keeping licenses current, and meeting your obligations to your customers. We're not liable for a missed renewal, lapsed warranty, re-treatment or delayed real estate closing caused by a reminder that wasn't sent or wasn't received.

8. Records and forms

Nuvolve helps you keep pesticide application records and fill in reports. Templates are a starting point. You're responsible for checking that the records and forms you use meet your state's current rules and any lender or industry form requirements (for example, wood-destroying insect reports), and for what your licensed applicators record.

9. Beta features and the founding shop program

Features marked "beta" are new and may change, break or be removed. They aren't covered by the SLA. If your company joined the founding shop program, the founding shop agreement sets out your free period, your price afterward and what each side commits to. Where it and these terms disagree, the founding shop agreement wins for your company.

10. Acceptable use

Don't use Nuvolve to break the law, send spam or messages people haven't agreed to, harass anyone, upload malware, try to get into another company's data, overload the service, or resell access. We may suspend an account that does, and we'll tell you why unless the law or an emergency stops us.

11. Availability and support

Our uptime commitment, support hours and reply times are in the Service Level Agreement. The live state of the service is on the status page.

12. Changes to Nuvolve and to these terms

We improve Nuvolve all the time and may change or remove features. We won't remove a core feature you depend on (scheduling, routes, the tech app, invoicing, payments, data export) without at least 90 days' notice. If we change these terms in a way that matters, we'll email the account owner at least 30 days before the change takes effect.

13. Our promises

The promises on What Nuvolve won't do (no selling or sharing your leads, no marketing to your customers, no cut of your payments, no ads in your app or portal, no holding your data hostage, no long-term contract, no training AI on your data, no selling your business data) are part of these terms. We won't weaken them for your account while you're a customer.

To the extent the law allows, neither side is liable for indirect or consequential losses (lost profits, lost business, lost goodwill), and each side's total liability under these terms is limited to the amount you paid Nuvolve in the 12 months before the claim. This cap doesn't apply to your payment obligations, to either side's fraud or willful misconduct, or to liability that can't be limited by law.

15. Disputes: talk first, then arbitration, no class actions

Please read this section carefully. It affects how disputes are decided. It means most disputes are decided by an arbitrator, not a judge or jury, and that claims are brought one company at a time, not as a class action. You can opt out within 30 days of signing up (see below).

  • Talk first. Before either side starts a claim, it sends the other a written notice describing the problem and what it wants, and both sides try in good faith to resolve it for 60 days. Notices to us go where section 16 says.

If it isn't resolved, any dispute arising out of or relating to these terms or Nuvolve will be decided by binding individual arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, by a single arbitrator. Unless both sides agree otherwise, it will be held by video, or in the county of our principal place of business. The arbitrator may award the same remedies a court could award to an individual party, within the limits of section 14.

  • No class actions. Claims may be brought only in a party's individual capacity, not as a plaintiff or class member in any class, collective, consolidated or representative proceeding. The arbitrator may not combine more than one party's claims.
  • Jury waiver. If a dispute goes to court for any reason, both sides waive a jury trial to the extent the law allows.
  • Opting out. You may opt out of this section by emailing support@nuvolvecrm.com within 30 days of the day your company first signed up, with your company name and the words "arbitration opt-out". Opting out doesn't change anything else in these terms.
  • If the class action waiver is found unenforceable for a claim, that claim goes to court, not arbitration, and the rest of this section still applies to every other claim.
