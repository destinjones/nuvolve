1. What you're signing up for

Nuvolve is software for running a pest control company: scheduling, routes, the technician phone app, billing, customer messages and the rest of what's listed on the pricing page. You get access to it for as long as your subscription is active. You don't buy the software, and we don't buy your business data.

The person who signs up must be able to bind the company, and must be at least 18. You're responsible for everyone you give a login to, and for keeping logins private.

2. Price, billing and automatic renewal

Your subscription renews automatically every month until you cancel. Each month we charge the card on file $39 for each route you run that month (at least one), plus any sales tax we're required to collect. You can cancel at any time in Settings, under Payments & plan, online, in a few clicks, without calling anyone.

  • Free trial. The trial lasts 14 days and needs no card. When it ends, nothing is charged unless you add a card and choose to continue.
  • What a route is. A route is a person on your team who runs a day of stops in a billing month, as defined on the pricing page. Office staff, managers and sales reps are not routes.
  • Price changes. We'll email the account owner at least 30 days before a price change takes effect. If you don't want the new price, cancel before it starts.
  • Taxes. Prices don't include sales tax, VAT or similar taxes. Where the law requires us to collect them, they're added to your bill.
  • Failed payments. If a charge fails, our payment processor emails you and retries. If it still can't be collected, the account is paused until it's paid. A paused account can still download all its data.

3. Canceling

  • Cancel any time in Settings, under Payments & plan. It takes effect at the end of the month you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.
  • We don't give partial refunds for the rest of a month you've already started, except where the law requires it or the SLA gives a credit.
  • After canceling, the owner can still log in and download everything, free. To have your company's data deleted, the owner emails us and we delete it; backups, including the off-site copy, age out within 30 days after that. See Your data.

4. Your data

Your business data, including your customers' names, addresses, service history and payments, is yours. You give us permission to store and process it only to run Nuvolve for you, keep it safe, back it up and support you. We don't sell it, rent it, or use it to market to your customers. The Data Processing Addendum is part of these terms and says this in the formal way: you are the controller of your customers' personal information, and we are your processor.

You're responsible for having the right to put your customers' information into Nuvolve and to contact them, including getting any consent the law requires before texting or calling them.

5. Payments to you

Money your customers pay goes through your own Stripe account, not ours. Stripe's terms govern those payments. Nuvolve doesn't hold your money and takes no cut of it. Card numbers are entered on Stripe's pages and never reach Nuvolve.

6. AI features (the receptionist, Ask Nuvolve, suggestions)

Some features use AI to draft replies, answer customers' texts, suggest bookings or answer questions about your numbers. These features are AI-assisted, and a person on your team can always check, change or undo what they do.

  • AI can be wrong. It can misunderstand a customer, suggest the wrong time, or describe a price incorrectly. You decide which AI features are on, what they're allowed to do, and what prices and services they may offer.
  • A price the AI quotes isn't binding on you unless you've set it as a price it may quote. Bookings it makes appear in your schedule and inbox, where your team can confirm or change them.
  • You're responsible for the messages sent from your account, including ones an AI feature drafted or sent on your behalf under the settings you chose.
  • We don't use your data or your customers' messages to train AI models, and we choose AI providers who agree not to either. See Subprocessors.

7. Reminders and renewals are best-effort

Nuvolve sends reminders: appointment texts, payment reminders, license and training expiry warnings, and termite warranty and contract renewal notices. We work hard to make them reliable, and the app shows what was sent and what wasn't. But texts and emails can be delayed, filtered or lost by carriers and inboxes, and a customer's contact details can be wrong.

Reminders help you keep track. They don't replace it. You remain responsible for renewing termite warranties and contracts on time, keeping licenses current, and meeting your obligations to your customers. We're not liable for a missed renewal, lapsed warranty, re-treatment or delayed real estate closing caused by a reminder that wasn't sent or wasn't received.

8. Records and forms

You're responsible for checking that the records and forms you use meet your state's current rules and any lender or industry form requirements (for example, wood-destroying insect reports), and for what your licensed applicators record.
