Changelog

What changed in Nuvolve, newest first. Small fixes are grouped; the full history is in the code.

November 1, 2026

  • Off-site backups. Every night's backup is now also uploaded to separate cloud storage, and the status page shows whether it arrived.
  • Public site. Draft terms, privacy policy, DPA, subprocessors and SLA; a status page; comparisons with FieldRoutes, GorillaDesk and PestPac; an ROI calculator; guides to state pesticide records; a glossary.
  • Spanish everywhere customers look. Invoices, service reports, offers, termite renewals, the booking form, the pay page and tracking pages follow the customer's language.
  • Money fixes. A Stripe outage is no longer treated as a decline, account credits apply to the next invoice, bounced bank payments are reversed properly, and a double-tap can't charge a card twice.
  • Security hardening before hosting: sealed secrets, lasting rate limits, platform admins confirmed by email, and a second security review.
  • "Today" is your company's day, not the server's, for routes, arrival texts and reminders.

October 31, 2026

  • Getting paid: Stripe webhooks, collections with retries, expiring-card notices, bank (ACH) payments and scheduled price increases.
  • Pesticide application records, state exports and a tamper-evident activity log.
  • Proof of service on every stop, and structured access notes (gate codes, dogs, call ahead).
  • Callbacks: re-service visits, free re-treat windows, the callback rate and how callbacks affect pay.
  • Profit: profit per stop, per route and per zip code, lead source value and payback.
  • Self-scheduling from real open times, cancel reasons with save offers, and Google review requests.
  • Texting compliance: consent per channel, STOP/START/HELP, quiet hours and 10DLC registration per branch.
  • Switch wizard in five steps, "Download everything" in Settings, and the public pricing, security and data pages.
  • The Nuvolve logo and app icons.

October 29, 2026

  • First release: customers, plans, schedule, routes, service tickets, invoices, the customer pay page, branches, business rules, insights and acquisitions.
  • Smart routing with drive times, live tech GPS and on-my-way texts; a tech app that works offline.
  • Termite and WDI, commercial accounts with sites and stations, door-to-door sales and contracts with the 3-day right to cancel.
  • Payroll and QuickBooks Online, inventory with truck stock, the document builder, the marketing hub and retention autopilot.
  • AI receptionist and text inbox, Ask Nuvolve, importing from FieldRoutes, GorillaDesk and PestPac, an open API and Zapier.
