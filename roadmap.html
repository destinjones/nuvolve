Roadmap

What's being built next, what's being considered, and what we've decided not to do. Founding shops have the biggest say in what moves up.

Building now

  • Rain-day reschedule: move a whole day's stops in one step
  • Vacation holds, skipped visits and seasonal frequency changes without canceling a plan
  • A high-contrast light mode for the tech app in direct sun
  • Sales tax by jurisdiction, with exemption certificates on file
  • Commercial billing: net terms, purchase orders and one statement across many sites
  • Guided first-route setup and a help center inside the app

Next

  • Lead feeds from Angi, Thumbtack and HomeAdvisor, and CallRail call tracking
  • Merge two customers or two properties after the fact
  • Label-rate warnings, re-entry intervals and PPE on the ticket
  • Same-tech preference and fixed weekdays by neighborhood in route planning
  • Wildlife and exclusion work orders, and damage or incident claims with photos
  • App Store and Google Play versions of the tech app, with push notifications

Not planned

  • Selling leads, advertising, or taking a cut of your payments. Never.
  • Long-term contracts

Want something that isn't here? Email support@nuvolvecrm.com or book a call. Shipped work moves to the changelog.
