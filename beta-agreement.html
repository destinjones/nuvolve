Founding shop agreement

What a founding shop gets, what it gives, and what happens at the end, in writing before anything starts. This agreement is between Nuvolve CRM ("Nuvolve", "we") and the pest control company accepted into the founding shops program ("you"). The Terms of Service and the Data Processing Addendum also apply; where they disagree with this page, this page wins.

The short version. Twelve months free, every feature, every route, no card. After that, $39 per route a month, locked for two more years, and never charged unless you choose to continue. You can leave any time, free, with all your data. We may only quote you with your written OK on the exact words.

1. The free year

  • When it starts. Your free year starts on the day your first technician runs a real route on Nuvolve (your "start date"), not the day you were accepted, so setup and the data move don't eat into it.
  • What's included. Every feature on the pricing page, for every route you run, for 12 months from your start date. Texting, card fees and other outside services are paid to those providers on your own accounts, as for every Nuvolve customer (for example, card fees go to Stripe in your own Stripe account).
  • No card, no automatic charge. We don't ask for a card during the free year. When it ends, nothing is charged unless the account owner adds a card and chooses to continue.
  • Reminders before it ends. We'll email the account owner 60 days and again 14 days before your free year ends, with your price and what to do to continue or leave.

2. Your price after the free year

  • Months 13 to 36: $39 per route per month, the list price when you joined, locked. If our list price goes up, yours doesn't. If our list price goes down, you pay the lower one.
  • From month 37: the list price at that time, with at least 60 days' notice of what it will be.
  • A route means the same as on the pricing page. Sales tax, where we're required to collect it, is added as a separate line, as section 2 of the Terms explains.
  • The lock is for your company. It stays if you add routes or branches, and it ends if you cancel and come back after your free year would have ended.

3. What you agree to

  • Run real routes. Use Nuvolve for your real work for at least 90 days from your start date. Running your old system alongside it for the first few weeks is fine, and expected.
  • Talk to us. A 20-minute call every two weeks for the first three months, at a time that suits you, then whenever you have something to say.
  • Tell us what breaks. Report problems through the Feedback button in the app, by email or by phone, especially on rain days, callbacks, renewals and month-end billing.
  • None of this is a penalty clause. If you can't keep up the calls, say so and we'll work around it.

4. What we agree to

  • Move your data in for you, from GorillaDesk, FieldRoutes, PestPac, another system or spreadsheets, free.
  • A direct line to the founder by phone, text or email during Mon-Fri 7am-6pm and Sat 8am-noon Central, with the reply times in the SLA.
  • Fix what's blocking you first. A problem that stops you running routes, billing or keeping state records goes ahead of new features.
  • Tell you what's coming. You see changes before they ship to everyone, and you'll hear from us before anything you use is changed in a way you'd notice.

5. This is a beta

Nuvolve is new. Things will break, and some features will change as we learn from you. Keep your own copy of anything you can't afford to lose; the free data export in Settings makes that one click. Because the free year has no fee, SLA service credits don't apply during it, but the SLA's support reply times do.

6. Leaving early

  • You can leave at any time, for any reason, with no fee, including in month four. Cancel in Settings or tell us.
  • Your data stays downloadable after you leave, free, exactly as in Your data.
  • The free months don't carry over to another company and can't be paid out.
  • If you leave and come back before your free year would have ended, you get the rest of it, and the price lock in section 2 still applies.
  • If you stop using Nuvolve for 60 days in a row without telling us, we may end your free year with 30 days' notice by email. You keep your data, and you can continue on the founding price in section 2.

7. What we may say about you

  • Nothing public without your written OK. We won't use your company's name, logo, photos or words on our site, in ads or anywhere public unless you've approved the exact words and where they'll appear, in writing (an email is fine).
  • You can take back that OK at any time for anything not yet published, and we'll take down what's on our own site within 10 business days.
  • We never share your business data (customers, prices, revenue, routes) in a testimonial or case study without your separate written approval of the numbers.
  • A testimonial is never a condition of the free year. Saying no changes nothing.

8. Your feedback

You're free to give us ideas, and we're free to use them to improve Nuvolve, without owing you anything for them. Using your idea doesn't give us any right to your business data, which stays yours under the Terms and the DPA.

9. If Nuvolve shuts down or is sold

If we shut Nuvolve down, we'll give you at least 90 days' notice and keep your export working until the end. If Nuvolve is sold, the buyer must honor your free year and the price lock in section 2. The continuity plan is linked from the help center.

10. Ending the program

We can close the founding shops program to new shops at any time. Closing it doesn't change anything for shops already in it.

Accepting

When you're accepted, we email the account owner a copy of this agreement with your start date. Replying "I agree", or running your first route after receiving it, accepts it. Questions first? Email support@nuvolvecrm.com.
