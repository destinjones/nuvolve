How we keep your company's data safe

Plain answers about where your data lives, who can see it, and what happens if something goes wrong. This page describes how the app works today.

Your own database

Every company gets its own database file. Each request is tied to one company before anything else runs, so another company's staff can't reach your customers, and yours can't reach theirs.

Links you send customers (pay page, signing, reviews) carry your company too, so they only ever open your records.

Encrypted on the way

The app runs over HTTPS and tells browsers never to fall back to plain HTTP. The login cookie only travels over HTTPS and page scripts can't read it.

Card numbers never touch Nuvolve. Customers type them on Stripe's page, and Nuvolve keeps only the card brand and last four digits.

Safe logins

  • Passwords are stored as scrypt hashes, never as text
  • Repeated wrong tries are slowed down
  • Two-factor login with any authenticator app, plus one-time backup codes
  • The owner can require two-factor for owners, admins and branch managers
  • Changing a password or turning on two-factor logs you out everywhere else

The right people see the right things

Owners and admins set things up. Branch managers see their branch. Office reps run the day. Technicians see their own route on their phone. Sales reps get the door map and their own deals, not the customer list.

Branch walls are checked by the server on every record, so staff tied to one branch can't open another branch's customers, even by guessing a link.

A record of who did what

The activity log keeps price changes, plan cancellations, payments and voids, rule overrides, team and role changes, password and two-factor changes, imports, undos and every full data export, with who did it and when.

Backups, checked every day

Every night each company's database is copied to a backup, and the last 14 days are kept. Every day the newest backup is opened on its own and checked: it has to pass SQLite's integrity check, and its customers, visits and invoices are counted against the live data.

Owners and admins see the last check in Settings, under Import & export. Restoring a backup is one command, and it sets the current files aside first.

Keys and connections

  • API keys are shown once and stored only as a SHA-256 hash
  • Each key carries only the permissions you pick, and can be held to one branch
  • Webhooks are signed (HMAC-SHA256) and only go to public https addresses
  • Your Stripe secret key never leaves the server

Your data is yours

The owner can download everything, any time, free, even after canceling: spreadsheets for every list and one JSON file, with photos if you want them.
