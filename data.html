Your data is yours. Take all of it, any time.

Your customers, their history and your records belong to your company, not to us. Getting them out is one click, it's free, and it always will be.

Our promise

  • The owner can download everything, any time, from Settings, under Import & export. One click, one .zip file, no form to fill in and no one to ask.
  • It's free. No fee to export and no fee to leave.
  • It works after your free trial ends and after you cancel. A paused account can still download everything.
  • Nothing is held back: customers, addresses, plans, every visit and service ticket with the products used, invoices, payments, estimates, contracts, documents, messages, notes and the activity log. Photos too, if you tick the box.
  • Spreadsheets anyone can open, plus the same data in one JSON file for developers and other software.
  • Want a copy without asking? Turn on the monthly email and the owner gets one every month, early on the 1st (over 10 MB, it's a download link instead of an attachment).

What's in the download

contracts/, documents/Signed contracts and the documents you sent, as pages you can open and print
photos/Visit photos, when you tick "Include photos"
README.txtWhat each file is, and how many rows each list has

Left out, on purpose

These would let someone into your account or your customers' pages, so they never go in a download:

  • Passwords, two-factor secrets and login sessions
  • Password reset links and the private links on customer pay, signing and review pages
  • API keys, webhook secrets and payment keys

Card numbers are never stored in Nuvolve. They stay in your Stripe account, and each customer's Stripe ID is in the download so you can find them there.

How leaving works

  1. In Settings, under Import & export, press Download everything. Tick Include photos if you want them.
  2. Open Customers.csv to check it. Most systems can import a customer list like it, and Service history.csv brings the history.
  3. Cancel any time in Settings, under Payments & plan. There's nothing else to sign.
  4. Want your data deleted too? Email support@nuvolvecrm.com from the owner's email address. Backups, including the off-site copy, are kept for up to 30 days, then they're gone.
