Getting started

How long does setup take?

Most owners get through setup in one evening: company details, work days, services and prices, techs, then the first route. The setup page shows what's left. If you're bringing customers from another system, add an hour or two for the import.

How do I move my customers over from GorillaDesk, FieldRoutes or PestPac?

Export your customers, plans and service history from your current software, then open Switch to Nuvolve and drop the files in. Nuvolve recognizes those exports' columns, shows a dry run first, flags duplicates, and can undo the whole import in one click. We'll help for free.

Do I need a Stripe account?

Only to take card and bank payments in Nuvolve. Customer payments go to your own Stripe account, so the money is yours directly and Nuvolve never holds it. You can run routes and send invoices before you connect Stripe, and record cash and checks by hand.

What's a route, for billing?

A route is a person on your team who runs a day of stops, like a technician, or you if you run the truck yourself. Office staff, managers and sales reps are free. The pricing page has the details.

The technician app

Do techs need to download an app?

Not from an app store. Techs open Nuvolve on their phone and add it to the home screen, where it works like an app. It keeps the day's route on the phone, so stops can be completed with no signal and sync when the signal comes back.

Does it work on Android and iPhone?

Yes, in Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone.

Money

Does Nuvolve handle sales tax?

Today you set one sales tax rate for your company, and Nuvolve adds it to invoices and shows it on the invoice and in your reports. Pest control tax rules differ by state and county, and sometimes between residential and commercial work, so check with your accountant which rate applies to your services. Separate rates by area and exemption certificates for tax-exempt customers are on the roadmap. Nuvolve's own subscription price doesn't include tax; where we're required to collect it, it's added to your bill.

Does Nuvolve take a cut of my payments?

No. Stripe charges its normal processing fee, and Nuvolve adds nothing. See what we won't do.

Does it sync with QuickBooks?

Yes, QuickBooks Online: invoices, payments and payroll entries.

Your account

How do I cancel?

In Settings, under Payments & plan. It's online and takes a few clicks; there's nothing to sign and no one to call.

Can I get all my data out?

Yes, any time, free, even after canceling: Settings, Import & export, Download everything. See Your data.

Is my data safe?

Each company has its own database, logins can require two-factor, and backups are made nightly, copied off-site and checked daily. The details are on the security page, and the live state of the service is on the status page.

What are your support hours?

Mon-Fri 7am-6pm and Sat 8am-noon Central. If you can't run routes, we aim to reply within one business hour; everything else within four. See the SLA.
