Privacy Policy

Nuvolve holds two kinds of personal information, and treats them differently: information about the pest control companies that use Nuvolve and their staff, and information those companies keep about their own customers.

The short version. If you're a pest control company, your customers' information is yours: you decide what goes in, and we only use it to run Nuvolve for you. If you're a homeowner or business that gets service from a company using Nuvolve, that company is in charge of your information. Ask them first; we'll help them answer you.

Who we are

Nuvolve CRM. Contact us about privacy at support@nuvolvecrm.com.

1. Your customers' information: the pest company is in charge

Pest control companies put their customers' information into Nuvolve: names, service addresses, phone numbers, emails, service history, notes, photos, signatures, invoices and payments, messages, and access notes a technician needs at the door, such as a gate code or "dog in the back yard".

Access notes are ordinary service information, protected like the rest of a company's data (its own database, staff roles, the activity log). They aren't the sensitive kinds of information the Data Processing Addendum asks companies to keep out of Nuvolve, such as health information, Social Security or other government ID numbers, and bank or card numbers.

  • For this information the pest control company is the controller: it decides what is collected and why. Nuvolve is the processor: we store and process it only on the company's instructions, to provide the service. The Data Processing Addendum sets out those duties.
  • We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, use it to market to those customers, or combine it across companies. Each company's data lives in its own database.
  • If you're one of those customers and want to see, correct or delete your information, contact the pest control company that serves you. If you contact us instead, we'll pass your request to them and help them answer it.

2. Information about pest control companies and their staff

For this information Nuvolve is the controller. We collect:

  • Account details: company name, address, phone, the owner's and staff members' names, emails and roles, and hashed passwords (we never store passwords as text).
  • Billing: the number of routes and your subscription status. Card details are handled by Stripe; we only see the card brand and last four digits.
  • Usage and security records: logins, the activity log, IP addresses and device type, used to keep accounts secure and to fix problems.
  • Location from the phone apps: while the technician app is open (and the technician hasn't paused sharing), the phone's location goes to their own company for dispatch and arrival times; the sales app uses location to place doors on the map. It is not shared outside that company, except that a customer waiting for a visit can see their technician's arrival time.
  • Support conversations by email or phone.

We use it to provide and secure the service, bill for it, support you, and tell you about changes to the service. We send product news only if you ask for it, and you can unsubscribe in one click.

3. Visitors to this website

If you fill in a form (the waitlist, the founding-shops application, the guides list, or a call request) we keep what you typed and use it only to reply to you and to send what you asked for. Joining a list sends one email asking you to confirm; nothing else goes to that address until you click it, and every email after that has a one-click unsubscribe link. If you give a phone number, we keep the consent words that were next to the box, and we only call or text you about what you asked for. Website statistics, where turned on, use Plausible Analytics, which doesn't use cookies and doesn't track you across sites. The demo on this site runs in your browser; anything you change in it is stored in your browser only.

4. Who else handles data

We use a small number of service providers to run Nuvolve, such as hosting, email and text delivery, maps, and AI features a company chooses to turn on. Each one is listed, with what it does, on the subprocessors page. They may only use the data to provide their service to us. We'll also disclose information if the law requires it, and we'll tell the affected company first unless we're legally barred from doing so.

5. AI features, automated texts and calls

When a company turns on the AI receptionist or Ask Nuvolve, the relevant messages or business data are sent to our AI provider to produce an answer. Our AI provider doesn't use API data to train its models. Neither do we.

  • The receptionist says it's automated. When a pest control company's AI receptionist texts you, its first message says it's an automated assistant, and you can ask for a person at any time.
  • Calls aren't recorded by Nuvolve. Nuvolve doesn't record phone calls. A company that records calls on its own phone system can have Nuvolve play a recording notice before the call rings through; if you don't want to be recorded, hang up and text the number instead.
  • Texts need your consent. The booking form and the customer portal ask before a company texts you, keep a record of what you agreed to, and let you change your answer. Reply STOP to any text to stop them all.

6. How long we keep it

  • A company's data is kept while its account is open, and after cancellation until the owner asks us to delete it. Backups, including the off-site copy, age out within 30 days.
  • Website form entries are kept until you ask us to delete them, or for two years without contact.
  • Security logs are kept for up to 12 months.

7. Security

How we protect data is on the security page. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell the affected companies without undue delay, and help them tell their customers where the law requires it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Virginia, Texas and other states with privacy laws), you may have the right to know what we hold about you, get a copy, correct it, delete it, and opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising. We don't sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. To make a request, email support@nuvolvecrm.com. We'll confirm who you are and answer within the time the law sets (usually 30 to 45 days). We won't treat you differently for asking.

9. Children

Nuvolve is for businesses and isn't meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the bottom and email account owners before it takes effect.
